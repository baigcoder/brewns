# Sound credits

`piano/` holds 18 notes of the **Salamander Grand Piano V3** by Alexander Holm,
licensed CC BY 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/), taken from
the `@audio-samples/piano-mp3-velocity6` package and trimmed to five seconds,
mono. The credit is shown in the sound panel.

Everything else (the room, the voices, the bar, the door and the road) is
synthesised in `lib/audio-ritual.ts`. A real café recording saved as
`cafe.mp3` in this folder replaces the synthesised room and bar.
