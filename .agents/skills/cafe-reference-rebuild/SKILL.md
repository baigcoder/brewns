# Café Reference Rebuild Skill

1. Read `PRD.md`, `RULES.md`, `DESIGN.md`, and `MEMORY.md` before implementation.
2. Separate every observation into `OBSERVED`, `INFERRED`, or `TARGET IMPLEMENTATION`.
3. Reproduce interaction behavior, not brand assets or copied text.
4. Prioritize the cup intro, 3D coffee studio, cursor-leaning cards, scroll clock, bean field, and thermal receipt.
5. Keep business content semantic and independent of canvas rendering.
6. Validate desktop, mobile, and reduced-motion behavior after each major section.
